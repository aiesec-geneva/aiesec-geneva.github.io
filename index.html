About AIESEC in Geneva

AIESEC is the world's largest youth-led organisation. In Geneva, we develop youth leadership through international volunteering opportunities, events for students and the experience of being an AIESEC member.

Before you apply

Frequently asked questions

Do I need previous experience to apply?

No. We look for motivation, reliability and a willingness to learn. You will receive guidance and support as you take on your role.

Can I apply to more than one department?

Yes. Tell us which areas interest you. During the selection process, we can discuss where your interests and strengths fit best.

What is the time commitment?

The commitment varies by role and time of year. We will explain the expectations before you join so you can decide whether they fit alongside your studies.
