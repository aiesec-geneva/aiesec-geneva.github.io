Our departments

Join one of our eight teams and gain hands-on experience while contributing to AIESEC's work in Geneva. Explore each department below to find the role that fits your interests.

01 / 08

Outgoing Exchanges

Join OGX and inspire global changemakers

The OGX department is dedicated to empowering young people (18 to 30 years old) to volunteer abroad through Global Volunteer (OGV), an international experience that allows them to contribute to impactful projects while immersing themselves in new cultures.

As part of OGX, you will:

  • Promote Global Volunteer: Help students discover the value of volunteering abroad.
  • Guide interested participants: Support them from their first inquiry to their departure.
  • Ensure a smooth journey: Help make their experience abroad enriching and hassle-free.

If you are passionate about global impact, cultural exchange and helping young people step out of their comfort zone, OGX is the place for you.

02 / 08

Incoming Exchanges

Join ICX and bring global talent to local companies

The ICX department (Incoming Exchange) connects companies with talented young professionals from around the world through internship programs designed to drive innovation and international collaboration.

As part of ICX, you will:

  • Promote international internship opportunities to local businesses and organizations.
  • Build strong relationships with companies to understand their needs and match them with the right candidates.
  • Ensure a smooth exchange process from contract negotiations to the intern's arrival and integration.

If you are passionate about networking, business development and creating global career opportunities, ICX is where you can make a real impact.

03 / 08

Membership Experience (HR)

Join MXP and shape the ultimate AIESEC experience

The MXP (Membership Experience) department is the heart of our community, ensuring that every member thrives, grows and feels valued within AIESEC. Our mission is to create an enriching and empowering journey for every individual in the organization.

As part of MXP, you will:

  • Lead recruitments: Bring in passionate and motivated new members.
  • Support and coach members: Help them grow through mentorship and guidance.
  • Organize skill-building workshops: Foster leadership, teamwork and personal development.
  • Strengthen AIESEC culture: Create an inclusive, inspiring and engaging environment.
  • Maintain alumni relations: Keep connections strong beyond AIESEC.

If you are passionate about HR, people development and creating an unforgettable team experience, MXP is where you belong.

04 / 08

Finance and Legalities

Join F&L and drive financial excellence in AIESEC

The Finance and Legalities (F&L) department is the backbone of AIESEC, ensuring the organization operates smoothly, transparently and sustainably. Aligned with AIESEC's global seven Finance Standards, F&L plays a crucial role in maintaining financial health and legal compliance.

As part of F&L, you will:

  • Manage budgets and financial planning to ensure efficient resource allocation.
  • Handle transactions and accounting for smooth financial operations.
  • Oversee legal compliance to ensure AIESEC operates within regulations.
  • Conduct audits and risk management to maintain transparency and security.
  • Work collaboratively on investments and financial strategy for long-term growth.

If you are interested in finance, accounting or law and want to develop essential skills in financial management, F&L is the place to gain hands-on experience.

05 / 08

Business Development

Join BD and build game-changing partnerships

The Business Development (BD) department is all about creating strategic partnerships that support AIESEC's initiatives and events. From securing sponsorships to collaborating with local businesses, BD plays a key role in enhancing the quality and impact of our activities.

As part of BD, you will:

  • Secure sponsorships and in-kind partnerships to fund and improve our events.
  • Engage with local businesses through collaborations and giveaways.
  • Research and reach out to potential partners via email, phone and direct meetings.
  • Represent AIESEC in networking spaces by pitching our vision and forging meaningful connections.

If you love negotiation, networking and business strategy, BD is where you can make a real impact while developing professional skills.

06 / 08

Engage with AIESEC (Project)

Join EwA and bring AIESEC to life on campus

The Engage with AIESEC (EwA) department is the creative force behind all AIESEC events at the University of Geneva. Our mission is to organize impactful events that promote AIESEC's values and the UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), inspiring students to take action.

As part of EwA, you will:

  • Plan and execute major events that engage and inspire students.
  • Develop essential skills in event management, logistics and problem-solving.
  • Collaborate with multiple departments including Marketing, Finance and BD to bring events to life.
  • Gain a global perspective on AIESEC while making a tangible impact on campus.

If you love event planning, teamwork and creating unforgettable experiences, EwA is the place for you.

07 / 08

Marketing and Communication

Join Marketing and Communication and shape AIESEC's voice

The Marketing and Communication department is the driving force behind AIESEC in Geneva's brand identity, ensuring our message reaches and inspires the right audience. We blend creativity with strategy to engage students through digital content, campaigns and promotional materials.

As part of Marketing and Communication, you will:

  • Manage AIESEC's social media presence on Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn and more.
  • Create engaging content from eye-catching graphics to compelling videos.
  • Develop strategic marketing campaigns to boost visibility and engagement.
  • Align global branding with local innovation for maximum impact.

If you are passionate about content creation, branding and digital marketing, this is your chance to bring your creativity to life and make a real impact.

08 / 08

Public Relations

Join PR and elevate AIESEC's presence

The Public Relations (PR) department is the bridge between AIESEC and the external world, shaping our public image and fostering valuable connections. Through media outreach, strategic partnerships and high-impact networking, PR ensures that AIESEC remains a recognized and trusted organization.

As part of PR, you will:

  • Build and maintain relationships with businesses, institutions and media representatives.
  • Manage AIESEC's public image through strategic communication and media outreach.
  • Create networking opportunities to expand our influence and partnerships.
  • Enhance AIESEC's credibility and visibility on a local and professional level.

If you are passionate about strategic communication, networking and brand reputation, PR is the place to develop your skills and make a lasting impact.
